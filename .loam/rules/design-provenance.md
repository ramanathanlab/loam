---
name: design-provenance
scope: all
enforced_by: none -- advisory
---

- A datum designed in this campaign — a candidate construct, a proposed
  parameter set, a generated variant — originates in the hypothesis and exists
  in no external source, so it must travel in full rather than as an identifier.
  Paste what was designed; retrieve what was published.
- When the hypothesis gives only a design concept and no concrete candidate, use
  a design tool to construct one or a few candidates realizing that concept, and
  write them to disk under the working directory. Do not invent a candidate to
  stand in for a design step.
